# Global Energy Research

Independent research on global energy markets, organised along the value chain: market foundations, upstream production, midstream logistics, downstream end use, regional geopolitics, policy and carbon, and financial analysis.

- `ENERGY ANALYSIS/`: dated section briefs (Markdown) and data workbooks (Excel).
- `Website/`: static site generated from the briefs by `Website/_build/build_site.py`. It is rebuilt and published to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.

Raw third-party downloads (IEA, OPEC, Energy Institute, EIA, etc.) are kept locally and not republished here. Every figure in the briefs is cited to its source.

*For research and educational purposes only. Not investment advice.*
