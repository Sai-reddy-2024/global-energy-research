05 Regional Markets and Geopolitics: November 2026 update

An earlier edition. The latest brief supersedes it.

Prepared 2026-11-10. Updates the 17 Oct brief. Background that has not changed is in the v01 brief. Inferred marks my own reading.

Timeline since 17 Oct

  • 30 Oct: Brent futures at $117.64.
  • 1 Nov: the seven core OPEC+ members hold December targets at 31.01 mb/d, the third month without change.
  • 3 Nov: US midterm elections. Markets start pricing a US–Iran deal.
  • 6 Nov: a tanker is mined in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • 9 Nov: COP31 opens in Antalya. Brent is near $93 after a 16% weekly drop.
  • 10–11 Nov: US–Iran talks with mediators in Abu Dhabi.

05.1 OPEC and allies

  • Crude output, June 2026 vs June 2025 (EIA): Saudi Arabia 7.29 mb/d (−26%), Iraq 1.95 (−56%), Kuwait 1.39 (−46%), Iran 3.15 (−19%), Qatar 0.48 (−63%). The UAE was at 4.17 (+13%) once it left OPEC and could export through Fujairah.
  • OPEC secondary sources show the recovery since then: Saudi Arabia at 8.0 mb/d in September (see 01 brief).
  • 29 Nov is the key date. All 21 OPEC+ members meet to set the 2027 framework, using a delayed independent capacity review due by mid-November (WarBrief, Reuters).
  • Inferred: if Hormuz reopens before 29 Nov, quota baselines become a live fight, because the UAE is outside the group and Saudi Arabia will want to recover market share.

05.2 North America

  • US crude output was 14.0 mb/d in the week to 30 Oct (+2.6% y/y), and EIA expects a record 14.3 mb/d in 2027. Canada produced 4.95 mb/d in June (+2.2%).
  • US refiners are running at 94.5%, and distillate stocks are 11% below last year (see 04).

05.3 Major demand hubs

  • Europe: gas storage was 72.4% on 8 Nov, the lowest for the date on record. TTF is about €82–86/MWh (about $27/MMBtu), up 164% y/y, and JKM is about $31. An early-December cold snap is a risk (EnergyReader, Morgan Downey).
  • China: oil consumption was 16.4 mb/d in 2024 (EIA, latest annual). China's monthly crude output was steady at 4.42 mb/d in June.
  • India: oil consumption was 5.6 mb/d in 2024 (+4%), the fastest growth among large markets.

05.4 Russia and Caspian

  • EIA's June 2026 estimate for Russian crude output is 9.58 mb/d (−2% y/y); some months in its Russia series are blank. Kazakhstan was 2.15 mb/d, flat y/y.

05.5 Latin America

  • Brazil was the standout grower at 4.47 mb/d in June (+19% y/y). Guyana reached 0.91 mb/d (+41%). Venezuela was 1.16 mb/d (+18%) under US licences.

05.6 Africa

  • Nigeria 1.69 mb/d (+3%), Libya 1.37 mb/d (flat) and Algeria 1.19 mb/d (+5%) in June.

05.7 Gulf LNG and Qatar

  • Qatar's crude and condensate output was 0.48 mb/d in June, a third of last year, which confirms how far the Ras Laffan complex was shut. North Field East first LNG is still expected in 1Q/1H27 (see 02 brief).
