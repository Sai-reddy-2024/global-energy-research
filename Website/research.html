  3. 03 · Midstream Logistics 10 Nov 2026

    Midstream Logistics and Transportation

    Seaborne gas is concentrated: the US, Qatar, Australia and Russia supply most LNG, and almost all of Qatar's 126 bcm has to pass through Hormuz. Europe (38% of world gas imports) and Asia (37%) compete for what is left.

    Downstream Refining and End Use

    US refiners are running hard: utilisation was 94.5% in the week to 30 Oct, against 86% a year earlier. Gross inputs were 17.1 mb/d (+9% y/y).
