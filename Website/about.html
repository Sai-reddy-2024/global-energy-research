Method

Data sources and licences

  • US Energy Information Administration (EIA): weekly petroleum and gas storage data, spot prices, Electric Power Monthly, international production and the Short-Term Energy Outlook. EIA data are public domain.
  • FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis: retail fuel prices, IMF primary commodity prices and US macro series.
  • CFTC: Disaggregated Commitments of Traders reports.
  • GIE AGSI+ (Gas Infrastructure Europe): EU gas storage. Data from agsi.gie.eu.
  • Ember: electricity generation and power-sector emissions from Ember Yearly Electricity Data, used under the CC-BY-4.0 licence.
  • Energy Institute: Statistical Review of World Energy 2026 (2025 country data).
  • Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas: Dallas Fed Energy Survey (shale breakevens).
  • Lazard: LCOE+ 2026.
  • Agency reports: IEA Oil Market Report, OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report, IMF World Economic Outlook, OIES Quarterly Gas Review, USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries.
  • Company data: annual reports, results releases and filings, with market values from companiesmarketcap.com. Every row in the company tables names its source.
