03 Midstream Logistics and Transportation: October 2026 brief

Prepared 2026-10-17. Inferred marks my own reading.

03.1 Maritime shipping and chokepoints

  • Hormuz: largely blocked or disrupted since 28 Feb 2026. Iran says the strait stays closed until the US accepts its seven-condition reopening plan (Al Jazeera, 4 Oct). In practice, crude is moving under restrictions: the IEA estimates 10.9 mb/d of Hormuz flows in September. Products and LNG are far more constrained.
  • Bab el-Mandeb / Red Sea: Houthi attacks resumed in October (IEA), which threatens the Yanbu (Red Sea) bypass outlet.
  • Tanker rates: the Baltic VLCC TC average was about $198k/day in July, $272k in August and about $449k in early September. It peaked near $723k/day on 18 Sep, compared with about $80k a year earlier. Values for 15-year-old VLCCs rose 61% between July and September (Cyprus Shipping News / S&P report).
  • Inferred: freight is a major part of delivered crude cost. Any de-escalation would hit tanker equities and asset values fast, as the S&P report itself warns.

03.2 Pipeline networks

  • Saudi East–West pipeline (Abqaiq to Yanbu, the main Hormuz bypass) was struck on about 11 Sep 2026 and shut down. Brent spot hit $131 on 15 Sep, and flows partly resumed by 22 Sep (EIA). Saudi exports still reached a wartime high in late September (CNBC).
  • UAE Habshan–Fujairah: existing bypass. A further UAE bypass pipeline is expected in 2027 (EIA).
  • Bypass pipeline exports totalled 5.6 mb/d in September (IEA).
  • Russia to Europe: pipeline imports were stable y/y in Q2 and Q3. The EU pipeline gas ban takes effect 30 Sep 2027, possibly slipping to 1 Nov (OIES).

03.3 Storage and terminals

  • EU gas storage: 75.8 bcm (71.6%) at end-Q3, 12.2 bcm lower y/y. It may miss 75% by 1 November, and end-winter stocks are expected to be low (OIES).
  • US gas storage: 3,523 bcf at end-September, heading to 3,850 bcf by end-October, about 2% above the 5-year average (EIA).
  • SPRs: the IEA announced a coordinated stock release on 18 Mar 2026. Total volumes still need to be confirmed from IEA and DOE releases.
  • LNG terminals: Ras Laffan has about 2.1 Mt of liquefaction online (Energy Aspects, Jun-26). The binding constraint is transit through Hormuz, not plant capacity. One report says repairs take three years; treat that as unconfirmed.

03.4 Trade flows and sanctions

  • Crude rerouting: about 103 mb of oil was added on the water in September as voyages lengthened (IEA).
  • Russia (CREA, Sep-26): fossil export revenue was about €625m/day. China took 55% of the top-five importers' purchases and India 25%. Of seaborne Russian oil, 57% moved on sanctioned shadow tankers. Urals averaged $90.7, a $23 discount to Brent. The EU/UK price cap is frozen at $44.10 under the 21st package, so it no longer binds in practice.
  • Iran: a US naval blockade has been in place since 13 Apr 2026, and US forces have struck IRGC-linked tankers.
  • Venezuela: US Treasury licences are in place, and 17 fields were handed to a US-backed company in August (see 05.5).
