How to read the KPIs

KPIDefinition (own words)Why it matters / how to read it
Production (kboe/d)Oil + NGL + gas volumes per day, gas converted at ~6 mcf per boe.Scale. Check whether figures are working-interest (equity), net entitlement (after royalties/PSC) or operated.
Liquids shareOil + NGL as % of total boe.Liquids earn more per boe than gas outside high-gas-price regions; drives realised price.
Proved reserves (1P)Volumes reasonably certain to be recovered under current economics (SEC/PRMS).Underpins valuation; SEC 1P uses 12-month average prices so it moves with price.
Reserve replacement ratio (RRR)Reserves added in the year / production in the year. Organic RRR excludes acquisitions and sales.>100% means the reserve base is growing. One year is noisy; use 3-year averages.
Reserve life index (R/P)Proved reserves / annual production, in years.How long current output can be sustained without new additions.
Finding and development (F&D) costCapex spent to find and develop reserves / reserves added, $/boe.Capital efficiency of growth. Compare with netback.
Lifting (production) costCash operating cost to produce one boe, $/boe.Floor on cash cost; TotalEnergies reports ~$5/boe (2025).
NetbackRealised price minus royalties, production cost and transport, $/boe.Cash margin per barrel.
Recycle ratioNetback / F&D cost.>1.5-2x indicates value-creating reinvestment.
Decline rateYear-on-year production loss from existing wells without new investment.Shale declines steeply (often >30% first-year); conventional fields far slower; drives maintenance capex.
Breakeven priceOil price needed to cover operating costs (existing wells) or to earn a return on a new well.See 02.4 workbook: Dallas Fed 2026 new-well average $66/bbl, existing wells $43/bbl.
PV-10Present value of future net cash flows from proved reserves at 10% discount (SEC standardised measure basis).Asset-value anchor for E&P valuation.
Uranium: tU / Mlb U3O81 tU = 2.6 thousand lb U3O8 (approx).Kazatomprom reports tU; Cameco reports Mlb U3O8.
Lithium: LCELithium carbonate equivalent; 1 t Li = ~5.32 t LCE; spodumene concentrate (SC6) ~0.15 t LCE per t.Brine producers report LCE; hard-rock miners report concentrate tonnes.

KPI framework informed by the private course reading (MM material, Ch.2 E&P); wording is original.
